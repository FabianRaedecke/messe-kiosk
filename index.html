Both sides cleaned without manual handling

The integrated Dry-Flip Station turns the mask in a dry state within the automated sequence. Frontside and backside can be processed in one run without manual intervention. This reduces the risk of contamination and damage and supports a stable, repeatable process flow. The 172 UV Unit activates the surface from hydrophobic to hydrophilic, enabling efficient particle removal and breaking up organic residues for further processing.

Your benefits:

  • Surface activation and preparation for efficient particle removal
  • Frontside and backside processing in one automated run
  • Lower handling risk for valuable masks
  • Stable and repeatable process flow

Flexible pre-cleaning for every mask type

The Pre-Cleaning Module reliably removes organic contamination and particles. It combines proven sulfuric acid/hydrogen peroxide mixture (SPM) processing and the patented, acid-free in-situ UV (ISUV) process in one module. Full-jet dispense, beam and megasonic cleaning, and frontside and backside rinse let you adapt the process to each mask type.

Your benefits:

  • Choice between proven SPM and acid-free in-situ UV processing
  • Reduced chemical consumption with the acid-free option
  • Compatibility with multiple process media for greater flexibility

Effective particle removal, gentle on fragile features

The optional Final Cleaning Module balances high particle removal efficiency with the protection of fragile mask features such as sub-resolution assist features (SRAFs). Beam and megasonic cleaning combined with droplet spray let you select the right level of physical force for each application. Frontside and backside rinse prepares a clean mask on both sides for inspection.

Your benefits:

  • Adjustable physical force for each layout and mask type
  • Clean frontside and backside before inspection
  • Compatibility with multiple process media

Controlled thermal processing for effective haze control

Residual ions such as sulfate and ammonium are a known root cause of haze on masks used in KrF (248 nm) and ArF (193 nm) lithography. In combination with a hot deionized (DI) water rinse, the hot plate provides a dedicated thermal step to reduce residual ions. The cool plate then brings the mask back to a controlled temperature for the next process step.

Your benefits:

  • Support for effective residual ion removal and haze control
  • Controlled thermal conditions for a stable process flow
